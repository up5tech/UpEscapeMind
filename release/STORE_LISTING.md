# Store Listing — Up Escape Mind

## App name

Up Escape Mind

## Short description (Android, ≤80 chars)

A moody offline escape-room mystery. One house. Dozens of locked secrets.

## Full description (EN)

Wake on the floor of the Meridian House. Every clock has stopped — each at
a different time. Someone arranged all of this for you.

Up Escape Mind is a single-player, story-driven escape-room adventure
that plays entirely offline. Explore five richly detailed areas of the
Clockmaker's Archive, collect items, decode clues, and unravel the secret
of the house that keeps what it forgets.

**Chapter 1 — The Clockmaker's Archive**
- 5 hand-crafted areas: Entrance Hall, Clockmaker's Study, Workshop,
  Portrait Gallery, and the Sealed Observatory.
- 19 puzzles across observation, hidden-object, keypad, rotation,
  sequence, mechanical, logic, and symbol-matching challenges.
- An inventory with item combinations, a Journal that collects every
  clue and secret, and a 3-level hint system that helps without ever
  blocking your progress.
- A chapter summary with a star rating for finishing, solving every
  puzzle, finding every secret, and playing without walkthroughs.

**Privacy-first by design**
- No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics.
- No internet permission on Android; nothing leaves your device.
- Your save lives only on your device.

Available in Vietnamese (default) and English.
